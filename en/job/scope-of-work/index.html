Licensure Scope of Work Code of Ethics

The Duty Concerning Prevention and Hygiene Management of Dental and Oral Diseases

Dental hygienists perform preventive oral-health services and support safe, effective dental care.

  1. Installation and removal of orthodontic arch wires
  2. Applying fluoride
  3. Oral radiography for diagnostic purposes
  4. Temporary filling of teeth
  5. Installation of temporary dental composites
  6. Removal of dental composites
  7. Removal of tartar from teeth
  8. Taking dental impressions

Other Professional Duties

Dental hygienists also perform other duties regarding prevention and hygiene management within the scope permitted by law.
