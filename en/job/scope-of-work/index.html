Licensure Scope of Work Code of Ethics

The duty concerning the prevention and hygiene management of dental and oral diseases

  • A. Work regarding the prevention and hygiene management of dental and oral disease according to the following classifications:

      • 1) Installation and removal of calibration arc wires

      • 2) Applying fluoride

      • 3) Oral radiography performed by a health or medical institution for diagnostic purposes

      • 4) Temporary filling of the teeth

      • 5) Installation of temporary dental composites

      • 6) Removal of dental composites

      • 7) Removal of tartar from the teeth

      • 8) Getting the dental impression

  • B. Any other duties regarding prevention and hygiene management of dental and oral diseases
