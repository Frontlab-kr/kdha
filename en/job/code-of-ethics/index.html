Code of Ethics

We contribute to human dignity and health through oral health education, preventive care, cooperation in dental treatment and professional practice.
  1. 1

    Abide strictly by the law and protect the professional dignity and social trust of dental hygienists.

  2. 2

    Commit to human oral-health enhancement beyond ethnic, religious and economic backgrounds.

  3. 3

    Respect each patient’s beliefs and protect personal information acquired during care.

  4. 4

    Refuse tasks not permitted by humanitarian law and cooperate with other health professionals.

  5. 5

    Continue research and learning to improve the quality of dental hygiene practice.

  6. 6

    Collaborate to improve the professional status and rights of dental hygienists.

  7. 7

    Maintain fair employment conditions and stand against inhumane practices.
