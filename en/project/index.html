Major Projects

01

Rights and working conditions

We focus on enhancing the rights and interests of dental hygienists through research and improvement of relevant laws, employment conditions, work environments, workforce supply and demand, and the professional dental hygienist system.

02

Development of abilities and education

KDHA surveys education, develops standard curricula and clinical practicum programs, improves the national qualification examination, and operates diverse continuing education programs.

03

Academic research

We encourage academic activity and information exchange through symposiums, research publications, professional books, support for affiliated societies, awards, and international participation.

04

National oral health

KDHA promotes national oral health through Oral Health Day, nationwide volunteer services, public education programs and accessible oral-health materials.

05

International exchange

KDHA became a full member of IFDH in 1989 and continues international academic and professional exchange worldwide.
