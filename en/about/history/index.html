The History of KDHA

2022

Held a temporary general meeting of delegates and sent a delegation to the IFDH International Symposium in Dublin.

2021

Opened an instructor course in dental hygiene ethics education and conducted community oral-health research.

2020

Published Ethics in Dental Hygiene, supported vulnerable groups during COVID-19, and created Teerong.

2019

Sent a delegation to the IFDH International Symposium in Sydney, Australia.

2017

Held the 39th Academic Conference commemorating the 40th anniversary.

2014

Opened the Korean Dental Hygienists Building in Seoul.

2010

Opened the cyber training center and participated in the national health promotion project.

2007

Celebrated the 30th anniversary of KDHA.

2004

Revised the Code of Ethics and launched the Ethics Committee.

1989

Became a full member of the International Federation of Dental Hygienists.

1982

Approved as an incorporated association.

1977

The Korean Dental Hygienists Association was founded.
